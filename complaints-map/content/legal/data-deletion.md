# How to Delete Your Data

**Salamahafifi Solutions / Sahkan**

Last updated: 29 July 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. You do not need to give a reason.

## Option 1: By WhatsApp

Send us a message on the same WhatsApp number you used to submit your case, containing the word **DELETE** or **PADAM**.

We will confirm receipt, verify that the request comes from the number on file, and complete the deletion.

## Option 2: By email

Email privacy@sahkan.my with:

- The subject line **Data Deletion Request**
- The name you used when submitting
- The mobile number you used when submitting
- Your Sahkan case number, if you have it

We may ask one verification question before proceeding, to make sure we are not deleting someone else's data.

## Option 3: In the app

If you have an account at aduan.sahkan.my, sign in and go to **Settings > Delete my data**. Confirm when prompted.

## What happens next

| Step | Timing |
|---|---|
| We acknowledge your request | Within 3 working days |
| We verify it came from you | Within 7 working days |
| We complete the deletion | Within 21 days of your request |
| We confirm in writing that it is done | On completion |

## What gets deleted

- Your name, telephone number, WhatsApp number and email address
- Your exact location details
- The full text of your case and any correspondence
- Any photographs, video, audio or documents you sent
- Your account, if you have one
- Your WhatsApp message history with us on our systems

## What we may keep

We will keep the following, and we want to be clear about it:

- **Anonymised case records.** We may keep a record that a problem of a certain type was reported to a certain agency in a general area, on a certain date, and how long it went unanswered. This carries no information that can be linked back to you.
- **Records we are legally required to retain,** for as long as the law requires.
- **Data already held by the agency you reported to.** That body holds its own record of your original complaint under its own retention rules. We cannot delete it for you. If you want it removed, you will need to contact them directly, and we will tell you who to contact.
- **Backups.** Your data may persist in encrypted system backups for up to 30 days after deletion, after which the backups are overwritten in the normal cycle.

## Things worth knowing before you delete

- If your case is still open, deleting your data means we can no longer follow up with the agency or update you, and the case will be closed.
- Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore data once it is gone.
- If you only want to stop receiving messages, you do not need to delete everything. Reply **STOP** or **BERHENTI** on WhatsApp instead, and your case stays open.
- If you only want your case taken off the public map, ask for that instead. Message us and we will remove the pin while keeping the case active and still following it up.
