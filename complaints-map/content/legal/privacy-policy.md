## 2. What this service does

Sahkan is for people who have already reported a problem to a government agency, local authority or service provider and have not received a satisfactory response.

You give us the details of your case, including the reference number the agency gave you. We record it, track how long it has gone unanswered, follow up with the agency on your behalf, and keep you informed.

We are not a government body and we are not affiliated with any agency.

## 3. What personal data we collect

We collect only what is needed to track and pursue your case.

**You provide directly:**

- Name
- Mobile telephone number, including your WhatsApp number
- Email address, if you choose to give one
- The name of the agency or authority you reported to
- The reference number (nombor rujukan) that agency gave you
- The date you reported it to them
- The location of the problem, which may be an address, a landmark or map coordinates
- The description of your case, and any photographs, video, audio or documents you attach
- Any further correspondence you send us about the case

**We collect automatically:**

- Technical data when you use the website: IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and the date and time of access
- Message metadata when you contact us on WhatsApp: your WhatsApp number, message timestamps, and delivery and read status supplied by Meta

**We do not ask for** identity card numbers, passport numbers, bank or payment details, or any information about your health, religion, political opinions or other sensitive categories. Please do not include these in a case description. If you send them anyway, we will remove them from our records where we reasonably can.

## 4. Why we use your data, and on what basis

| Purpose | Basis |
|---|---|
| To receive and record your case | Your consent, given when you submit |
| To contact you about your case, including status updates and requests for more information | Your consent, and performance of the service you asked for |
| To follow up with the agency you reported to, quoting your reference number | Your consent, given when you submit |
| To publish anonymised information about unresolved cases (see section 6) | Our legitimate interest in public reporting, subject to the safeguards in section 6 |
| To maintain the security and integrity of the service | Our legitimate interest |
| To comply with a legal obligation, court order or lawful request | Legal obligation |

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

## 5. WhatsApp messaging and consent

If you contact us on WhatsApp, you are choosing to start that conversation. We will reply about your case.

- We only send WhatsApp messages relating to a case you have submitted, or in reply to a message you sent us.
- We do not send promotional, campaign or bulk messages.
- You can stop messages at any time by replying **STOP** or **BERHENTI**. We will stop sending messages, though this may mean we can no longer update you on your case.
- WhatsApp messages are delivered through Meta Platforms, Inc. and are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms. We do not control Meta's handling of message data on its own systems.

## 6. What we publish, and what we never publish

When you submit a case, it appears on our public map. The purpose of that map is to show how many people have reported a problem in a given place and how long it has gone unanswered. It is not to publish your report.

**We publish only:**

- The general location of the problem, rounded to street level rather than an exact address
- The category of the problem
- The name of the agency it was reported to
- The date it was first reported to that agency, and the number of days it has been outstanding
- How many people have reported the same problem
- The current status
- Photographs of the problem itself, but only after a member of our staff has reviewed each one and confirmed that faces, vehicle registration plates, house numbers and any other identifying detail are not visible. Photographs are never published automatically.

**We never publish:**

- Your name
- Your telephone number, WhatsApp number or email address
- Your exact address or exact coordinates
- The reference number the agency issued you
- The text of what you wrote to us
- Your Sahkan case number, which is sent only to you
- Any photograph in which you or your household are identifiable
- The content of your correspondence with us

If you would prefer that your case does not appear on the map at all, tell us when you submit or at any time afterwards, and we will remove it. Your case will still be followed up.

## 7. Who we share your data with

We share personal data only in these circumstances.

**The agency you reported to.** Following up on your case means contacting them and quoting your reference number. This may include your name and contact number, because most agencies will not discuss a case without confirming who filed it. If you do not want your details passed on, tell us, and we will follow up without them where the agency permits it.

**Other agencies**, where your case turns out to be the responsibility of a different body and needs referring on. We will tell you when this happens.

**Service providers who operate our systems on our behalf:**

- Meta Platforms, Inc., for WhatsApp message delivery
- Supabase, for database hosting and authentication
- Cloudflare, Inc., for website hosting and email routing

These providers act on our instructions and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

**Where the law requires it,** for example in response to a court order or a lawful request from an authority with power to make it.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

## 8. Transfers outside Malaysia

Our database is hosted in Japan. Our website hosting, email routing and messaging providers operate globally and may process data in other countries.

Where data is transferred outside Malaysia, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives a comparable standard of protection, including through the contractual terms we have with those providers.

## 9. How long we keep your data

- **Open cases:** for as long as the case is open.
- **Closed cases:** for 24 months after closure, so that we can respond if the problem recurs or you ask for the history.
- **After that:** we delete your personal data, or anonymise the record so that it can no longer be linked to you, and retain only the anonymised case information.
- **WhatsApp message logs:** 12 months.
- **Website technical logs:** 6 months.

If you ask us to delete your data sooner, see section 11.

## 10. How we protect your data

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), row-level access controls in the database, tiered staff permissions so that most personnel cannot see contact details at all, authenticated access, and logging of administrative actions. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps proportionate to the sensitivity of the data.

If a personal data breach occurs that poses a significant risk, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and will notify affected individuals where required.

## 11. Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** it if it is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date
- **Withdraw consent** to our processing, at any time
- **Limit** how we process your data
- **Request deletion** of your data
- **Request a copy** of your data in a portable format, where technically feasible

To exercise any of these, email privacy@sahkan.my or message us on WhatsApp. We will respond within 21 days.

Deletion has its own page with step by step instructions: https://aduan.sahkan.my/data-deletion

Please note that withdrawing consent or deleting your data may mean we can no longer pursue an open case on your behalf, and that information already held by a government agency remains subject to that agency's own retention rules and is outside our control.

## 12. Children

This service is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us, contact us and we will remove it.
